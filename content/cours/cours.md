# Course outline and material

## Lecture 1: Introduction and the notion of hierarchy of models

```{admonition} Monday, September 21, 2026

- Mathematical modeling of complex multiscale reactive dynamical systems
- Hierarchy of models and complexity
- Review of a set of application cases ranging from population dynamics in biology to combustion and flame dynamics, through excitable media, fluid mechanics, solar physics, etc... 

>  Petite classe associated with the lecture: [PC1 - The theory of thermal explosion](../pc/pc01_intro.md) 
```

- Applications in population dynamics and mechanics
