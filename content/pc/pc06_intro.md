# PC 6

The Petite Classe is divided into three parts. First, we come back to the notion of invariant manifold, which we study on toy examples to better grasp the properties and importance of these objects. Next, we discuss sufficient conditions for establishing the existence of certain bifurcations, generalizing some of the conditions already seen in the course, and apply them to some examples studied in the previous PC. Finally, we take a closer look at center manifolds, and study their links with bifurcations.
